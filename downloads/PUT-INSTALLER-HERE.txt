Drop your Windows installer in this folder named exactly:

    WinShot-Setup.exe

That's the file the "Download" button on the site links to
(downloads/WinShot-Setup.exe).

Prefer hosting the installer elsewhere (e.g. GitHub Releases)?
Edit the two download links in ../index.html — search for "WinShot-Setup.exe"
and replace the href with your release URL. You can then delete this folder.
