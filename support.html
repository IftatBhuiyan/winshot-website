Support

A little help,
when you need it.

Questions about the app, your account, or a purchase? Get in touch directly.

Email support

support@getstillmark.com

Report an issue

In Stillmark, right-click the tray icon and choose Report an issue. Review the diagnostic summary, then choose Email report. Add what happened, the steps to reproduce it, and what you expected before sending. Reports go directly to the support email above.

If you use webmail, copy the summary and paste it into a message. The app does not send reports automatically.

What to include

  • For a purchase or refund: the purchase email and order identifier from your receipt or account.
  • For account or activation help: the account email, device name shown in the license manager, and a short description of what happened.
  • For a technical issue: the app version, Windows build, steps to reproduce the issue, and the privacy-safe diagnostic summary copied from the app if available.

Do not email payment card numbers, sign-in codes, passwords, license keys, screenshots, recordings, or clipboard contents unless the publisher asks through a verified reply. For a refund, send the request within 30 calendar days of purchase; see the Refund Policy for the terms.
