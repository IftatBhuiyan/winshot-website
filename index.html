A few things
worth knowing.

Do I keep the app after a year?

Yes. Versions released during your included update year stay yours to use. There is no subscription or automatic renewal.

Can I move to another computer?

Yes. Deactivate the old computer in your account, then sign in to Stillmark on your new one and choose “Activate this computer.” Your license covers one computer. An offline computer learns about deactivation at its next successful check; its existing offline access lasts at most 30 days.

Where do my screenshots go?

Your captures and OCR are processed on your Windows computer. Save to a folder you choose, copy to your clipboard, or send to another app. A folder you choose may itself be cloud-synced.

Can I try Stillmark first?

Yes. Your 14-day trial starts when you first open the app. No account or payment card is required. After the trial, a valid license is required for capture and recording. The public download will open after final release checks are complete.
