Refund policy

30 days to change your mind.

Updated September 21, 2026. Every paid Stillmark license includes a 30-day money-back guarantee.

A full refund, for any reason.

Email your request within 30 days of purchase. Public sales are not open yet; sandbox purchases involve no real charge and have nothing to refund.

Our guarantee

If you buy the Stillmark one-time license, you may request a refund within 30 calendar days after the purchase, for any reason. After confirming the purchase, we will refund the full amount paid for that license, including taxes collected on the purchase, to the original payment method. We do not deduct payment-processing or restocking fees from your refund.

How to request it

  1. Use support@getstillmark.com or Contact support. Include the purchase email and, if available, the order identifier from your receipt, and state that you are requesting the Stillmark refund. Do not send a payment card number, sign-in code, password, capture, recording, or license key through an unverified channel.
  2. Submit the request within 30 calendar days after the purchase date. The time you send the request determines whether it is within the 30-day window, not the time we reply. Keep a copy of your request.
  3. We will confirm the request and, when eligible, submit the refund through the payment provider. The provider’s processing time may affect when the credit appears on your statement.

What happens after a refund

After a confirmed refund, the account license is marked refunded and access to licensed downloads in your account and further activations are blocked. You must stop using the refunded license and delete the licensed application and copies you are not otherwise entitled to keep. Files you created or saved remain on your computer until you delete them. The app may not learn of a refund until its next online check; continued offline validation during its remaining offline allowance of at most 30 days is not permission to avoid the refund’s effect.

Payment and statutory rights

Refunds are sent to the original payment method when the payment provider permits. We do not ask for your full card number. Nothing in this policy limits a non-waivable consumer refund, warranty, cancellation, chargeback, or other statutory right. If a law gives you a longer period or a different remedy, that law controls. A payment dispute should be used in good faith after you have given us a reasonable opportunity to resolve the request, except where your law or payment provider says otherwise.

Contact and status

The available contact paths are Contact support and support@getstillmark.com. This policy applies to paid Stillmark purchases when sales open. It was updated September 21, 2026.
