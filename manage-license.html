License help

License actions belong in the app.

The production order portal and recovery route will be added only after the provider flow is configured and tested.

Never paste a license key into this website.

This static preview does not collect, validate, recover, or store license keys.

Current application steps

  1. Open the tray menu.
  2. Choose License…
  3. Review the trial or activation status.
  4. Paste a valid key into the app to activate, or choose Deactivate to remove the stored activation from this PC.
