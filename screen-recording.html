Screen recording

Some things are easier to show.

A short walkthrough. A hard-to-describe bug. Record the part of your screen that tells the story, then trim it down to the moment that matters.

01 / Record

Keep your attention on the screen.

Choose an area and start recording. A compact control bar keeps pause and stop close by while you work. Choose microphone or system audio when your setup supports it.

02 / Trim

Leave the extra seconds behind.

Preview the recording and move the start and end handles to keep the useful part. Export options stay tucked away until you need to adjust them.

03 / Share

A file, ready to go.

Save the result to your computer, then drag the recording into the conversation or app where it belongs.

Recording is implemented in the current development app. Packaged-build audio, export, and hardware compatibility checks are still underway before public release.
