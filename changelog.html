Every detail, improving.

New features, refinements, and fixes for Stillmark.

1.0.0 · Available now

Released September 26, 2026. Download the signed Windows installer and start your 14-day free trial.

Capture, then carry on.

  • Capture an area, window, display, or all displays. Use a timer or scrolling capture when needed.
  • Annotate with arrows, text, shapes, highlights, blur, and numbered steps. Copy, save, pin, or drag the result into another app.
  • Record your screen and capture text using Windows OCR.
  • Reopen recent captures from History, and keep Stillmark ready in the tray.
  • Try the app for 14 days from first launch, without a payment card or account.

Support within reach

Choose “Report an issue” from the tray menu to review a diagnostic summary and open an email draft. You decide what to include and when to send it.

Your app, connected

Purchase a license through your Stillmark account, activate one computer, and transfer your license when you change computers. Settings, History, and Account share one app window. Open PNG and JPEG images from Windows directly in the editor. Stillmark checks for eligible signed updates and asks before downloading and restarting.
