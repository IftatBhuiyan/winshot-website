Features

One capture flow, several ways to finish.

Start from the tray or a configurable shortcut. Choose a mode, refine the result, then copy, save, drag, pin, or open it.

Capture

  • Selected area, window, current monitor, and all monitors
  • Timer and repeat-last-area
  • Text capture using installed Windows OCR language packs
  • PNG or JPEG screenshot output

Refine

  • Arrows, lines, rectangles, ellipses, freehand marks, and highlights
  • Text and numbered steps
  • Blur, pixelate, spotlight, and crop
  • Bounded undo and redo, then clipboard or rendered image export

Quick Access

  • Copy, save, reveal, open, annotate, pin, or drag
  • Browse recent image captures according to local history settings

Recording — release validation

Selected-area MP4, GIF conversion, audio options, recording controls, and trimming exist in the current source and remain subject to the packaged hardware/device matrix.

Resident workflow — release validation

Tray lifetime, configurable global shortcuts, launch at sign-in, and bounded shortcut recovery are implemented and remain subject to installed session/power validation.

Explicitly not current features

Cloud upload, hosted share links, automatic secret detection, webcam overlay, and guaranteed capture of protected/elevated content are not advertised.
